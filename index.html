Xinyi Dong

I am a first-year M.S. student in Computer Engineering at UC San Diego. I received my B.Eng. in Artificial Intelligence from Peking University.

My research focuses on reasoning in large language and vision-language models, and on data-centric methods for improving it. I have worked on teaching small language models to learn from their own mistakes through self-reflection, and on social reasoning from video, where models infer people's beliefs, intentions, and emotions. I am also interested in computational cognitive modeling, using Bayesian models to understand how people learn from social interaction.
